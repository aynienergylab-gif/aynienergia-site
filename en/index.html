The meaning of Ayni

Ayni is a principle of reciprocity found in Andean cultures. It is the practice of helping and giving back, strengthening the bonds between people and the community.

At Ayni Energia, this idea shapes a view of the energy transition that values cooperation, knowledge sharing and care in the relationship between society and nature.
