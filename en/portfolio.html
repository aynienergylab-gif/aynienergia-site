Energy planning begins with an understanding of the territories.

The challenge

Energy dynamics differ across regions, economic sectors and social groups. Understanding these particularities requires integrating energy, economic and territorial information, taking into account the productive and institutional characteristics of each context.

Scope of work

Energy planning in the public sector, development of and support for applied research, preparation of technical studies, and collaboration with organizations such as Oxfam Brasil and Agenda Pública, in addition to academic research.

Case 1 · Energy Directorate, SEDE-MG · 2025

A portrait of the Minas Gerais electricity sector based on public data sources

The challenge

Data on generation, installed capacity and electricity consumption in Minas Gerais existed, but were spread across different databases, with different concepts, units and reference periods. Without consolidating them at the state level, it was difficult to assess plans and policies and to gauge the state's role in the national electricity system, of which Minas Gerais accounted for 9.8% of generation in 2024.

The approach

The portrait followed the logic of Brazil's National Energy Balance (BEN), adapted to the state level. Regional data were collected from official databases, systematized and consolidated into historical series, structuring the electricity mix, installed capacity and consumption by consumer class, with breakdowns by source and by sector. The socioeconomic dimension, which relates consumption, economic activity and GDP, was brought in through indicators such as electricity intensity.

The result

A report, a database and a digital dashboard, all publicly available. The Overview consolidates the electricity mix of Minas Gerais and the evolution of electricity generation and consumption, and connects with other studies by the Directorate, such as the one on energy efficiency in industry.

Case 2 · Energy Directorate, SEDE-MG · 2026

Where industry in Minas Gerais has the most to gain from efficiency

The challenge

Industry accounts for 52.39% of electricity consumption in Minas Gerais, compared with 35.19% in Brazil, and this demand is concentrated: metallurgy and mining account for 58.9% of the state's industrial electricity consumption. What remained was to identify where energy efficiency has the greatest potential and which policy instruments can support it.

The approach

The analysis examined the evolution of industrial consumption by segment and the electricity intensity of industry. Because electricity intensity alone does not measure technical efficiency, it was read together with the sectoral composition and the weight of electro-intensive segments. From this, priority segments and activities were identified, and technologies, management practices and policy instruments were surveyed, relating efficiency, industrial competitiveness and reductions in consumption and emissions.

The result

A study with recommendations for state planning, accompanied by outreach materials and consultation tools. It continues the series begun with the Guide for Municipalities on Energy Efficiency in Public Buildings.

Case 3 · Energy Directorate, SEDE-MG · 2026

From a set of studies to an agenda: the Low-Emission Technologies Program

The challenge

The Energy Directorate had studies, databases, guides, regulatory frameworks and initiatives produced at different times and without a common framework. This made it hard to show the coherence of the work, set priorities and connect the energy agenda to the state's economic development goals.

The approach

The program was structured in four stages: identifying the available studies and materials, surveying the regulatory instruments and related policies, grouping the content into strategic pillars and organizing the deliverables. The pillars are Energy Intelligence and Planning, Energy Efficiency, Low-Emission Technologies and Fuels, and Economic Development and Investment Attraction.

The strategic dimension

The program was designed to go beyond an inventory. By placing planning, efficiency, low-emission technologies and investment attraction within the same framework, it links energy policy to the state's economic development agenda and allows each study to be read as part of a whole. This organization also makes the gaps between the pillars visible, which helps decide which studies and instruments should come next.

The result

A document with an executive summary that brings the Directorate's deliverables together in a single agenda and gives institutional visibility to what had already been done.

Case 4 · Oxfam Brasil · 2025

The energy transition in the Legal Amazon, seen through its territories and people

The challenge

How are the impacts, risks and opportunities of the energy transition distributed among the territories, peoples and communities of the Legal Amazon?

The approach

The methodology combined Theory of Change and analytical matrices applied to five themes: bioeconomy, access to energy, socio-environmental inequalities, traditional peoples and communities, and climate governance. The evidence base brought together technical documents, scientific literature, public data and institutional reports.

The result

Contribution to the chapter on climate and energy transition of Encruzilhada Climática (Oxfam Brasil, 2025), with recommendations for public policy and advocacy.

Case 5 · Agenda Pública · 2025

Jobs of the future in territories that depend on oil, mining and agriculture and livestock

The challenge

How can territories that depend on oil, mining and agriculture and livestock move through the transition without losing jobs and local economies?

The approach

The research built the conceptual and methodological framework and analyzed labor microdata (RAIS, CAGED and PNAD), with data from IBGE and references from the ILO, ECLAC, the IDB and IPEA. It compared vulnerabilities, local capacities and pathways for diversification and productive reconversion.

The result

Final report, institutional presentation and executive summary, with recommendations. The research included the development of an analytical index to compare territories.

Case 6 · Energy Directorate, SEDE-MG · 2025

Guides to support municipal managers

The challenge

Municipalities need technical guidance translated into the routines of public administration, on energy efficiency in buildings and on solar energy.

The approach

The public buildings guide organizes references on energy management, diagnosis, audits, monitoring and procurement. The solar guide brings together context, concepts and practical guidance to identify opportunities in public buildings, analyze contracting and financing models and develop local policies.

The result

Two public guides: Energy Efficiency in Public Buildings (February 2025) and Photovoltaic Solar Energy and Energy Solutions (September 2025). The solar guide also supports the preparation of a Municipal Energy Development Plan.
